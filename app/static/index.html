See what a policy change changes.

RuleTrace uses local Gemma 4 to propose source-linked rules. You review each interpretation, then a deterministic Python evaluator compares borrowing requests under both policies.

Supported concepts: equipment item limits, duration in days, and training prerequisites. Unsupported clauses remain visible and prevent definitive outcomes.

Compare UTF-8 .txt policies up to 2 MB each, including insurance or other policy documents. Gemma notes cover selected changes only. Equipment-rule extraction supports short policies up to 6,500 bytes; insurance eligibility is not evaluated. Blank case inputs are unknown. Reviews are held in this server's memory for up to two hours.

This is a decision-support prototype, using fictional demo data. AI interpretations are not automatically correct.
